Prairieville questions, answered

I'm a one-person business. Is Starter enough?

Often, yes. Starter is an information website of up to 5 pages with a custom design, contact form, mobile and SEO basics, and 30 days of support. If you later need a blog or more pages, you can grow into Business.

Can you help with my Google Business Profile?

Yes. Google Business Profile setup is part of the SEO work that comes in every build.

What does a monthly plan cover?

Daily off-site backups, uptime and error monitoring, security updates, fixes, and the changes you ask for. On Grow and Partner, we also keep your domain and hosting renewals paid and current, with those costs billed at cost on top, and add a monthly strategy call and your Google profile and reviews looked after; Partner adds hands-on work each month. Billed monthly, and you can cancel or change with 10 days' written notice and no fee.
